Peptides & Health

A new review in Current Heart Failure Reports examines how GLP-1 medicines, combined with exercise and targeted nutrition, improve both body weight and muscle function in people with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) — a condition that's notoriously hard to treat.

Plain-language takeaway: GLP-1 drugs may do more than slim you down; paired with resistance training, they could preserve the muscle mass that heart patients most need. The authors flag exercise as non-negotiable — the drug alone isn't enough.

Read the review on PubMed — Not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before changing any treatment plan.
