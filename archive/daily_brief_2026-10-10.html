Plain-language takeaway: Cellular reprogramming — the idea of resetting aged or diseased cells toward a younger functional state — is moving out of the lab and into human trials faster than most people realise. Glaucoma is a high-value proof-of-concept: if a one-shot intervention can arrest nerve damage in the eye, the same toolkit becomes credible for other degenerative conditions. Early days, small numbers, but worth following. Not medical advice.
