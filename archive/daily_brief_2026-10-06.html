Peptides & Health

A new review in Peptides journal examines the bidirectional pharmacology of GIP receptor targeting in obesity — the same receptor pathway that underpins dual-agonist drugs like tirzepatide. The key plain-language takeaway: activating the GIP receptor can both reduce appetite and improve fat metabolism, but blocking it may also help in certain metabolic contexts. Researchers warn that translating these mechanisms from animal models to humans remains tricky, and individual responses vary widely. Not medical advice — speak to your doctor before changing any treatment.
