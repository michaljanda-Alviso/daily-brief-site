Artificial Intelligence

For You

Peptides & Health

A new clinical trial examining GLP-1 weight-loss drugs is now measuring effects beyond the scale — researchers are tracking whether these medications reduce substance use disorders and improve employment and housing stability. Early signals are promising. Plain-language takeaway: GLP-1 drugs may be doing something systemic to reward-seeking behaviour in the brain, not just appetite. If you're already on one, the downstream effects on motivation and habit formation are worth discussing with your doctor. Not medical advice.

AI — Your Move Today

McKinsey is now using AI simulations in job interviews. Practice speaking out loud with an AI today — open ChatGPT or your preferred model, describe a real work problem you've faced, and ask it to push back as a sceptical client or interviewer. Five minutes of verbal sparring will expose gaps in your reasoning that typing never does.

Investing — Where to Look

Two diverging signals worth tracking: defence tech (Anduril's $90B private valuation) is attracting serious capital even as broader AI hardware names (CoreWeave, Dell) are selling off sharply. The thesis: investors are rotating from speculative GPU-debt plays toward companies with locked-in government contracts. Watch the spread between neocloud bonds and defence-sector equity — if it widens further, it suggests the market is pricing a slower enterprise AI ramp than bulls assumed. Risk: any geopolitical de-escalation or defence budget squeeze could hit names like Anduril hard and fast. Not financial advice.
