For You

Peptides & Health

GLP-1s and bone health — a new concern to track. A systematic review published this week in Archives of Osteoporosis examined the skeletal effects of GLP-1 receptor agonists in adults with overweight or obesity. The findings are mixed: while metabolic benefits are well-established, the evidence on bone density and fracture risk remains inconclusive and warrants longer-term study.

Separately, a meta-analysis in the European Journal of Clinical Pharmacology found GLP-1 agonists significantly reduce antipsychotic-related weight gain and metabolic disruption — a potential new use case beyond obesity and diabetes.

Not medical advice. Discuss any medication questions with your doctor.
