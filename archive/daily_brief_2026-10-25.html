For You

Peptides & Health

GLP-1 agonists and schizophrenia: promising but early. A new systematic review and meta-analysis finds that GLP-1 receptor agonists — the class behind Ozempic and Wegovy — may reduce metabolic side effects in patients on antipsychotics for schizophrenia spectrum disorders. Weight gain from antipsychotics is a major reason people stop taking them, so a drug that counters that effect matters. The analysis is encouraging but the authors flag that trial sizes remain small. Not medical advice — talk to a clinician before drawing personal conclusions.

Separately, STAT News reports the race is on to expand mRNA cancer vaccines beyond their first proven tumor type, with Merck and Moderna presenting new data at ESMO this week. Worth watching if you follow oncology closely.
