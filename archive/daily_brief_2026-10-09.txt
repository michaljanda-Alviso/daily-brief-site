First, health research. A study published this week in a leading heart failure journal examines the combined role of exercise, nutrition, and glucagon-like peptide one medicines in patients with a specific form of heart failure known as heart failure with preserved ejection fraction. This is a condition where the heart pumps normally but the muscle is too stiff to fill properly, and it has historically been very difficult to treat. The research suggests that when glucagon-like peptide one receptor agonists, the same class that includes the widely known weight-loss drugs, are combined with structured exercise and dietary intervention rather than used alone, the improvements to body composition and muscle function are meaningfully greater than what medication achieves by itself. This matters beyond just the heart failure context. It adds to a growing body of evidence that these drugs work best not as standalone solutions, but as one component of a broader health approach. If you or someone you know is using this class of medication for any reason, the signal here is clear: the therapy appears to amplify what exercise and good nutrition do, rather than replace them.

Second, artificial intelligence, your move today. The L M Arena leaderboard story from this morning points to something you can use right now. The organization is expanding its evaluations to specifically test whether models are honest under pressure, which means the leaderboard is becoming a more reliable guide than it used to be for choosing which model to trust for high-stakes tasks. Today, go to the L M Arena site and run the same task through two or three competing models in blind mode, meaning you see the outputs before you know which model produced them. Pick a task that matters to your actual work, something where accuracy and straightforwardness are important. Rate the outputs honestly before the model identities are revealed. Doing this even once will sharpen your intuition about where your current model of choice actually sits versus its competitors. As the revenue reality behind Open A I becomes clearer and the model landscape shifts, having your own benchmark for what works in your workflow is more valuable than trusting any single provider's marketing.
