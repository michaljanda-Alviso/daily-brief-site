Completing this kind of plant in under two years is a significant engineering milestone, and the company says future phases will connect to the grid even faster.

First, a health and research signal worth your attention. This week, the journal Science published a special issue focused entirely on women's health, with significant attention on chronic pain and menopause. One of the clearest takeaways from the researchers involved is that chronic pain in women has been systematically understudied, and that hormonal transitions like menopause interact with pain pathways in ways medicine is only beginning to map. What this means in plain language is that if you or someone you know experiences chronic pain that fluctuates with hormonal cycles, the science now supports that experience as biological and real, not psychological. Researchers are calling for pain studies to specifically track hormonal status as a variable. It is not a treatment yet, but it is a shift in how the medical community frames the question, and that matters for the conversations patients should be having with their doctors.

Second, your artificial intelligence move for today. Open artificial intelligence's new virtual try-on feature inside Chat G P T is live right now. Here is what you should actually do with it today. If you work in e-commerce, fashion, personal styling, or retail marketing, open Chat G P T, upload a photo of yourself or a model image you have rights to, and test how the virtual try-on behaves with three or four different product types. Even if retail is not your world, running through this feature gives you a concrete feel for how multimodal artificial intelligence is moving into commercial workflows. That firsthand knowledge is worth more than reading ten articles about it.

Armadin's two-and-a-half-billion-dollar valuation at seed and early growth stage tells you that institutional money is pricing in a sustained wave of artificial-intelligence-driven attacks.
