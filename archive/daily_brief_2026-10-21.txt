On peptides and health research, a bipartisan group of lawmakers has sent a formal letter urging the Food and Drug Administration to close a regulatory loophole that is being used to import knockoff versions of retatrutide, the investigational obesity drug in development at Eli Lilly. Retatrutide is a triple receptor agonist targeting three hormonal pathways simultaneously, which in early trials produced significantly greater weight loss than existing G L P one drugs alone. The concern here is not about access to the legitimate compound, which is still in trials. It is about compounding pharmacies and importers exploiting a gray zone to sell unregulated versions to consumers before the drug has completed safety review. The plain language takeaway is this: if you are watching the obesity drug space, pay attention to how the F D A responds to this pressure. The loophole the lawmakers want closed is the same one that let compounded semaglutide scale rapidly during the shortage. If the agency acts swiftly, it could affect the broader compounding market just as next-generation peptides start entering the pipeline.

For your artificial intelligence move today, here is a specific, concrete action you can take right now. Google just announced that Gemini's Personal Intelligence feature is coming to cars, but you do not have to wait for the car version to put the same capability to work. Open Google Gemini on your phone today and connect it to your Google Workspace or personal Google account if you have not already done so. Then run a test prompt that combines calendar context with a real decision, something like: what do I have today that might conflict with a drive to the airport at three in the afternoon, and what should I prepare before then. The goal is not just to see what Gemini knows. It is to build the habit of giving your artificial intelligence assistant full context before asking it questions. Most people use these tools in isolation. Connecting them to live personal data is where the productivity gap opens up between light users and power users. Do that setup this morning while the commute is fresh.
