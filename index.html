For You

Peptides & Health — GLP-1s and Your Lungs

A new review in The European Respiratory Journal examines GLP-1-based therapies in lung diseases, finding emerging evidence that GLP-1 receptor agonists may have anti-inflammatory effects in the airways — potentially relevant for asthma and COPD alongside metabolic benefits.

Plain-language takeaway: If you or someone you know uses a GLP-1 drug primarily for weight or blood sugar and also has a respiratory condition, this is a conversation worth having with a doctor — the benefits may extend further than the label says. Not medical advice.
