The News

For You

Peptides & Health

GLP-1 rebound — what a new trial says about keeping weight off after you stop.

A pilot randomised controlled trial published recently on preventing weight regain after GLP-1 therapy discontinuation found that structured lifestyle intervention — combined with a defined tapering protocol — significantly slowed rebound weight gain compared with cold-stop discontinuation. The plain-language takeaway: stopping a GLP-1 drug abruptly appears to be the worst strategy; a gradual taper paired with calorie awareness and resistance training gave participants the best shot at holding their results.

Not medical advice. Talk to your prescriber before changing any GLP-1 dosing.

AI — Your Move Today

Set up a Gemini work agent trial in Google Workspace.

Google Cloud's new Gemini agent is rolling out to enterprise accounts now. If your organisation runs Google Workspace, open the Admin Console today, navigate to Apps > Google Workspace > Gemini, and enable the agent for a small pilot group. Give it one repeatable task — weekly report drafting or meeting-notes summarisation — and measure time saved over two weeks before rolling out wider.

  • AI safety tooling: Goodfire's commercial pivot signals that enterprises will soon budget explicitly for model-monitoring. Early-stage safety infrastructure is a nascent but growing line item in AI budgets.

Rising Treasury yields remain the single biggest macro risk to high-multiple tech valuations — do not ignore the bond market's warning. Not financial advice.
