Peptides & Health — GLP-1 Rebound: What the Pilot Data Says

A new pilot randomised controlled trial in Obesity Science & Practice tackled one of the biggest open questions in GLP-1 pharmacology: what stops the weight coming back after you stop the drug?

  • Researchers tested structured behavioural and dietary interventions bridging the discontinuation window.
  • Early results suggest that timed support — not just stopping cold — meaningfully reduces rebound weight gain.
  • The trial is small (pilot-scale), so treat the effect sizes with caution.

Read the abstract on PubMed. Not medical advice. Talk to your prescribing clinician before changing any GLP-1 protocol.
